PART 2 - PRIVACY POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Short version: Melodia has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash
reporting. Your library stays on your computer.

1. Data stored on your computer
   Melodia saves your playlists, favorites, equalizer settings and the paths
   of local audio files you add in one file, library.json, in:
       %AppData%\Melodia
   Melodia reads the audio files you choose only to play them and to read
   their tags (title, artist, album). This data is never sent to the Author.

2. Data sent over the internet
   To search and play music, Melodia contacts Audius servers
   (api.audius.co and discoveryprovider.audius.co hosts). Audius receives
   what any web request contains: your IP address, your search words, and
   the tracks, artists and albums you request. Audius handles that data
   under its own privacy policy: https://audius.co/legal/privacy-policy
   Album art and audio are loaded from the same services.

3. Optional API key
   If you set the AUDIUS_BEARER_TOKEN environment variable, Melodia sends
   that token to Audius to raise rate limits. It is read from your
   environment and is not stored or shared by Melodia.

4. No sale or sharing of personal data
   The Author does not receive, sell or share your personal data, because
   Melodia does not send any to the Author.

5. Children
   Melodia does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

6. Your control
   Uninstalling Melodia lets you choose whether to delete library.json. You
   can also delete the %AppData%\Melodia folder yourself at any time.

7. Changes
   If this policy changes, the new text ships with the new version.

8. Contact
   Privacy questions:pacifickimana@gmail.com
